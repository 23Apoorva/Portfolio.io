ABOUT Me

Frontend Developer

Hello! I'm Apoorva, a recent BE(ECE) graduate from PES College of Engineering, Mandya. Throughout my academic journey, I have developed a strong foundation in programming languages such as Java and Python, alongside web technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and Tailwind CSS. I consider myself a natural leader, having successfully managed my project group during college. Additionally, I've organized two impactful awareness programs: one focused on women's empowerment and another on mosquito awareness. In my pursuit of learning and growing as a developer, I've built several projects, including Amazon and Spotify clones, as well as various landing pages like a coffee shop page. I also enjoy creating games like Simon and Tic Tac Toe using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I am multilingual, with proficiency in Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, and English, which allows me to connect with diverse audiences and work effectively in different environments.
